# The Australia map

## Geography
<!-- type: fact; tags: map, spawn, naval; when: any; source: resources/maps/australia/manifest.json -->
The map shows Australia and its surrounding seas: 1000 × 750 tiles in the compact version, about 328,000 of them land. Most of the land is one continent; Tasmania is an island off the south-east coast and can only be reached by boat.

## Nations of Australia
<!-- type: fact; tags: map, nations, spawn; when: spawn, early; source: resources/maps/australia/manifest.json -->
Seven nations start at fixed places: Western Australia (west), Northern Territory (north), South Australia (south of the centre), Queensland (north-east), New South Wales (east), Victoria (south-east) and Tasmania (on its island in the south-east). The east and south-east are crowded with four nations close together; the west has only one.

## Start in open plains
<!-- type: strategy; tags: spawn, map, expand; when: spawn; status: to evaluate -->
Pick a start on plains and at least 100 tiles from the nearest nation: you get several free expansion waves before meeting anyone, and plains are the cheapest land. The centre and the north-west fit; the crowded south-east does not.

## Coast or inland
<!-- type: strategy; tags: spawn, naval, map; when: spawn; status: to evaluate -->
A coastal start allows ports and boats later, but an inland start has more land around it to expand into. Prefer room to grow over coast.
